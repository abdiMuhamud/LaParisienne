Notre histoire

A little piece of Paris,
rooted in The Gambia.

For more than three decades, La Parisienne has been where Gambian families mark the moments that matter — the birthday cake, the Sunday croissant, the long lunch with friends.

Our pâtissiers layer, glaze and pipe by hand, while the kitchen serves everything from steak frites to benachin. Three branches, one standard: every dish a masterpiece.

  • Celebration cakesBirthdays, weddings & naming ceremonies
